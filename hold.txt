Option A — Per-conversation context only (moderate complexity):
The bot remembers what THIS specific customer said earlier in THIS
conversation (e.g., "earlier you said your order number is #1234,
so I'll reference that now"). This is standard chat-history-in-
context, likely already partially possible if conversation history
is passed to the AI call today — worth checking if it already does
this or starts fresh every message.

Option B — Per-user PROFILE that persists across conversations
(significant complexity): The bot remembers facts about a specific
customer (via phone number) across DIFFERENT conversations over
time — e.g., "this customer previously complained about late
delivery two weeks ago." This requires a new data model (something
like a per-contact "memory" or "notes" table), explicit logic for
what gets stored and when, and real decisions about data retention/
privacy for customer conversation history — this is genuinely a new
feature, not a config tweak.
